One proposed deviation from the mock: input borders

The reference form fields use the hairline colour (1.24 to 1.36:1) with only a faint fill against the card (1.1:1). WCAG 2.2 asks for 3:1 on the boundary of a control. I propose a separate field border token, #738b79 in light and #6d8a76 in dark, each measuring at least 3:1 against the ground, the card and the soft fill in its theme (checked in the Phase 1 preview’s token generator and again on rendered pages). The first value I published, #7f9084, was only checked against two of the three surfaces and is superseded. It changes one visible detail and keeps the palette.

4 · Audit

The approved references next to today's running app

Today's screens come from the dev database's seeded Alliance organization, read-only, at 1440 px. The kiosk was captured at 1280 by 800.

Landing and academy registration

  • PLATFORM_NAME is still "[Platform name TBD]". It has one definition; four files import it (manifest, root metadata, logo mark, staff top bar) and the platform layout mentions it in a comment.
  • The home copy says "payments". The brief wants dues and payment-status tracking, since nothing collects money.
  • Registration is one 12-field form in a max-w-sm column with plain bordered inputs, no step indicator and no autocomplete attributes. Slug availability is checked with unguarded async calls, so a slower earlier response can overwrite a later one. Errors and the slug status are not announced to assistive technology. Full-height layout uses 100vh.
  • The server action uses a strict schema (z.strictObject), so a multi-step form must still submit every field in one final request, and the honeypot must stay.

Sign-in

  • No password visibility toggle, no autocomplete tokens, and the error line is not a live region. UX Pro Max flags all three under accessible authentication.

Student portal

  • Everything is stacked: progress, today's classes, history, promotion history, payment, schedule. The paginated history is most of the page height.
  • The weekly schedule is clipped on the right at desktop width (only Sunday to Tuesday visible).
  • Warning: the reference shows "20 / 60" and "40 more to the next stripe". That is correct for Alliance today because it is still on cumulative accounting until activation, and wrong for organizations on per-interval accounting. The design must render both from data and never fix a denominator.

Staff dashboard

  • The branding reminder banner sits above the numbers. The brief asks for it to be quieter.
  • "Students to contact" renders every match (no limit in contact-list.ts). It should be capped with a link to the filtered student list, with the filter in the URL.
  • On phones the sidebar collapses; the reference shows no admin navigation at 390 px.
  • Alliance's stored branding is a dark-green sidebar with an orange active item. That interacts with the tenant-palette decision below.

5 · Decisions I need from you

Eight choices the references do not settle

D1 How do tenant colours coexist with MATROOM?

B. Keep today's mechanism, change the default (recommended). Organizations with configured branding keep it; an organization with none gets MATROOM forest. Platform-owned pages are always MATROOM. Least behavior change, and it preserves tenant palette configuration as the brief requires. Alliance keeps its stored green and orange until its owner changes it. Say if you want Alliance reset to the default.
A. Platform chrome everywhere. Tenants keep name, logo and one identity accent only. The most consistent look, but it removes colour control tenants have today.
C. Hybrid. Tenant colour drives actions only; neutrals, ground and type stay MATROOM. Contrast is validated with the existing theme.ts.

D2 Editorial serif

Georgia stack, as in the mock. No files. It renders as Georgia on Windows, macOS and iOS but as a different serif on Android and Linux, so it will not match the approved look everywhere.
Vendor an open-licence serif (recommended). Same approach as the fonts commit: unmodified files, licence and source documented, no Google request at build time. I would put two or three candidates in a specimen next to the reference for you to choose, and check ₡ and Spanish accents before any is proposed. I have not picked one.

D3 App-surface type

Use IBM Plex Sans alone for portal, dashboard and kiosk, semibold for headings, and keep IBM Plex Mono only for codes and times. That retires Archivo, which saves about 104 KB of the current 401 KB first-load fonts, and it matches the app reference's plain sans headings. Confirm or keep Archivo.

D4 Dark theme for app screens

The app is dark-capable today and the brief says to keep both themes. The landing reference supplies dark values; the app reference has none. I propose reusing the landing's dark pairs (every text pair above clears 4.5:1) and reviewing real dark screenshots at each phase.

D5 Field border token (3:1)

Approve the small deviation described in section 3.

D6 Staff navigation on phones

The reference has none. I propose the existing sidebar toggle in the top bar (a sheet on phones, as far as I can see), with no invented bottom navigation. It is reviewed on screen before merge.

D7 Landing copy and content

Fictional sample students, ranks and classes in the product preview (the reference's names resemble the seeded Alliance data and are not publication consent); "dues tracking" instead of "payments"; a footer with only real destinations (sign in, register, language) and no legal links, since no legal pages exist. Approve the fixture and copy before the landing PR.

D8 Where the design authority lives

Copy the reference files into the repo (design/matroom/) with a README stating that they are the visual authority and which prototype-only elements must never ship, and add a superseded note to docs/REDESIGN_BRIEF.md. The brief requires this so future work does not revert the design. Impeccable's PRODUCT.md and DESIGN.md are optional; I would generate DESIGN.md at the end from the shipped result, not before.

7 · First implementation scope

Phase 1: identity and foundation

One pull request that makes the platform MATROOM and lays the design system down, without redesigning any screen. It is the smallest change that every later phase depends on.

Included

  • PLATFORM_NAME = "MATROOM" and its comment; the four importers and the one comment audited; "[Platform name TBD]" removed from production surfaces and messages.
  • A reusable code-native mark and wordmark, favicon and PWA icons, manifest colours from the tokens. No domain or trademark claim.
  • Light and dark tokens from section 3, wired through the existing shadcn variable layer, plus the field border token.
  • Fonts per D2 and D3, vendored the same way as the current ones.
  • Button, Input and a new labelled Field, Card, Pill, Badge, stat tile, table and empty state restyled with every state (default, hover, focus, active, disabled, loading, error).
  • The tenant mechanism adapted per D1 (with B: default changes, overrides untouched).
  • The /dev/components page updated as the living showcase.
  • A unit test that fails if any token pair drops below its target ratio, using the repo's existing contrast function.

Not included

  • Any page composition: landing, registration, sign-in, portal, dashboard and kiosk keep their layouts and change only through tokens.
  • Any server action, domain function, permission, migration or message text other than platform-name copy.
  • New dependencies, a Next.js upgrade, or CI changes.

Main risk

Changing tokens repaints every page at once. Mitigation: a before and after screenshot matrix of every route (English and Spanish, light and dark, phone and desktop, a default-branded and a configured tenant), the full test suite and smoke, and the semantic status colours (ok, warn, bad) re-measured on the new grounds before merge.

Acceptance for Phase 1

Every documented pair clears its ratio in both themes; the token test passes; no page loses a control or a state; all tenant-branding tests pass unchanged; Build and Parity are green on the pushed head; the browser matrix shows no clipped text in Spanish; fonts served from the repository with no Google request.

8 · Preserved behavior

What the redesign must not change

Attendance and promotion

  • One counted attendance per Costa Rica day; promotion resets per interval, and Alliance stays cumulative until activation. Progress views render both from data, never a fixed denominator.
  • Check-in window: start minus 30 minutes to end plus 30 minutes, inclusive; portal row states open, checked in, opens at HH:mm, closed; the page refreshes at the next boundary and the server stays authoritative.
  • Promotion confirmation, void and correction permissions and audit trails.

Kiosk and offline recovery

  • No class open writes nothing; one open checks in; several show the picker; cancelling writes nothing; the correction link; the abandon timer.
  • The offline queue with event ids, and unattributable replays kept as review evidence.
  • Staff review of queued check-ins: record on the original day with the confirmed tap time, or set aside with a reason.

Registration and tenancy

  • Strict payload, honeypot, rate limits, pending request then invitation, recorded terms version. No claim of instant access, a trial, a price or a turnaround.
  • Tenant isolation, role gates and branding scope; the platform name in one constant; no Alliance name on platform pages.

What pins this today

  • Component tests: kiosk-class-picker, kiosk-success-view, todays-classes-card, attendance-history-section, academy-choice, menu-cross-links, nav-items, theme.
  • portal-gates-on-linked-student, forged-claim-server-components, organization-registration, and messages-key-parity for English and Spanish.
9 · Verification plan

Responsive, accessibility, and English/Spanish checks

Responsive

  • 390, 768 and 1440 px for every screen; 320 px reflow check.
  • Kiosk at 1280 × 800, 800 × 1280 and 1024 × 768, with no scrolling.
  • Light and dark on every screen.
  • No horizontal page scroll; tables and the calendar scroll inside their own container.

Accessibility

  • Keyboard-only run of each flow; focus visible and never hidden by a sticky bar.
  • Labels, autocomplete tokens, announced errors and status, step changes moving focus.
  • Automated contrast for every token pair and 3:1 field borders.
  • Targets of at least 24 CSS px on the web and larger on touch surfaces; kiosk keys stay as large as today.
  • Reduced motion; 200% zoom. Lighthouse through the browser tools, so no new dependency. No screen reader is available here, and I will say so rather than claim one.

English and Spanish

  • Both locales for every changed screen, including worst-case Spanish labels in the stepper, tabs, tiles and buttons.
  • Accents, inverted punctuation and ₡ rendered from the vendored fonts.
  • Dates and numbers through Intl; every string in messages/; the parity test stays green.

Real flows, real users

Registration is tested end to end against the dev database and cleaned up afterwards: public form, approval script, invitation, a student signing up publicly and being approved in the staff UI. Nothing runs against production, and nothing is claimed as browser-verified unless it was.
