Controls and states

Buttons, fields, checkboxes, status, loading and navigation focus in both themes, with and without a tenant’s colours.

Overview1 · Identity & type2 · Student portal3 · Staff dashboard4 · Phone navigation5 · Controls & states

Illustrative sample data. Names, classes, counts, dates and the “Harbor Jiu-Jitsu” tenant are invented for this preview. Progress numbers and states come from the app’s real getAtBeltSummary and buildProgressView, run on throwaway students in both accounting modes. No testimonials, adoption figures or legal text appear anywhere.

Focus is shown with a forced .is-focus class that applies the same rule as :focus-visible (2 px outline, 2 px offset, ink colour; on the sidebar it uses the sidebar’s ink so it stays visible on a tenant’s dark sidebar). Field borders use --field (≥3:1). Disabled controls are marked by fill, text and cursor, and are exempt from contrast.
