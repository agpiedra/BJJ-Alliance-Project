What to approve

  1. The palette, including the new --field control boundary and the status colours (page 1, tokens table).
  2. Dark values: the landing’s dark palette is used as the starting point; the audit below checks every pair on the surface it actually sits on.
  3. Tenant treatment (Option B): the tenant’s colour replaces the action colour, sidebar and data fill; neutrals stay MATROOM. One correction shown here: in dark, a tenant’s button gets a 1 px --field edge because a mid-tone fill can sit under 3:1 against the dark card.
  4. Type: IBM Plex Sans for every Operate surface (retires Archivo), Georgia-stack serif for Persuade surfaces until D2 vendors a serif.
  5. Phone navigation: top bar + menu button + sheet for both student and staff; two new strings (menu, close menu).

Contrast audit on rendered surfaces: 1792 measurements on 38 rendered stages; 0 below threshold (0 distinct); 84 disabled-control measurements exempt. Details: reports/contrast-report.md.

What this is not

No app file, token, component or route was changed. No testimonial, adoption figure or legal document appears. Tenant “Harbor Jiu-Jitsu” is fictional. Georgia (the Persuade serif) is a system font: screenshots were taken on Windows; other platforms substitute.
