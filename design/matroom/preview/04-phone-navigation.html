MATROOM Phase 1 · visual preview for approval · proposal only, nothing here is in the app

Navigation at phone width

Student and staff navigation at 390 px: a top bar with a menu button and a sheet, using the app’s existing structure. No bottom navigation is invented.

Overview1 · Identity & type2 · Student portal3 · Staff dashboard4 · Phone navigation5 · Controls & states

Illustrative sample data. Names, classes, counts, dates and the “Harbor Jiu-Jitsu” tenant are invented for this preview. Progress numbers and states come from the app’s real getAtBeltSummary and buildProgressView, run on throwaway students in both accounting modes. No testimonials, adoption figures or legal text appear anywhere.

Frames are exactly 390 px wide and use container queries, so the same CSS answers to a real 390 px viewport (screenshots of the portal and dashboard pages at 390 px are in screenshots/). Open states show the sheet with its scrim. Menu and close labels are the only two strings the app does not have yet.
